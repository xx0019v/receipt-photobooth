# Premium Design Direction

Applying `premium-product-design` (SKILL.md HEAD `3e88c03`) → visual-direction §1 requires **one** concept sentence, not a trend collage.

## Three internal concepts (evaluated)

| Concept | Idea | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · PRECISION ARCHIVE** | storage machine, registration, serial, grid, archival labels, mechanical calm | operation clarity, product identity | can read as austere/cold, low drama → template risk returns |
| **B · KINETIC EDITORIAL** | paper cuts space, oversized type, asymmetric composition, moving crop, fashion-publication energy | originality, template-resistance, "world-class" drama, uses the physical paper as hero | must not sacrifice operation clarity |
| **C · SILVER APPARATUS** | silver mechanical primitive, optical instrument, frame indexing, calibrated light | signature FRAME CORE / ISSUE CORE identity | if over-used becomes "chrome blob" the brand forbids |

## Selection

**Primary: B · KINETIC EDITORIAL** (≈80%). It most directly answers the owner's complaint ("second-rate template feel") because it attacks the *grid* — full-bleed asymmetry and cropped oversized type break the shared `title→content→buttons` stack that makes every screen look alike.

**Accessory: A/C registration rigor (≤20%)** — every kinetic composition is *anchored* by one precision element (an oversized registration numeral, a crop/registration mark, or the silver core) so the drama still reads as a *machine issuing an edition*, not a fashion magazine. This keeps operation clarity (A) and the silver signature (C) without letting either dominate.

## Concept sentence

> **THE RECEIPT feels like a precision editorial press cutting a numbered print out of empty white space — expressed through asymmetric, edge-anchored grids built on one oversized registration numeral, condensed display type cropped hard against the frame, and interactions that lock, cut, and feed paper instead of fading.**

## Shared visual grammar (every screen inherits, none repeats the layout)

- **The anchor numeral** — each screen has exactly one oversized numeral (edition no. / frame count / selected count / serial / countdown) as its typographic hero, placed *off-center* against an edge, never a centered title.
- **Edge-anchored asymmetric grid** — content aligns to a left or right rail and bleeds toward one edge; whitespace is a deliberate mass on the opposite side, not uniform padding.
- **Registration hairlines** — thin rules + crop marks carry structure instead of boxes/cards.
- **Paper is the hero** — on Format/Proof/Printing/Done the artefact is the largest element; UI (labels, CTA rail) is subordinate and edge-docked.
- **One motion vocabulary** — TYPE SET · REGISTRATION LOCK · PAPER CUT · FRAME CORE · ISSUE CORE · PAPER FEED · COLLECT SETTLE. No generic fades; reduced-motion keeps state, drops travel.

## Per-screen composition intent (no two share a grid)

- **Idle** — silver hero centered in a field of white; minimal type at the four margins (protected; unchanged).
- **Edition** — one folio per scroll-scene: giant edition numeral top-left, name cropped, character line, issuing rail full-width bottom.
- **Format** — the live artefact at print scale is the hero; the other format waits as a physical edge-sliver; swipe/tap to swap (already rebuilt by owner — freeze).
- **Pose** — viewfinder dominates; the frame-count numeral is the header hero at right; one instruction line inside the frame.
- **Capture** — camera surface fills; the shot counter is oversized display type top-left; a single thin contact-strip of 6 at the bottom.
- **Registering** — FRAME CORE at installation scale center; count under it; no loader chrome.
- **Frame Selection** — hero proof (660px) with neighbour slats at the edges; selected-count numeral opposite the title; arc thumbnail rail; PRINT ORDER strip; peel-to-slot.
- **Proof** — artefact ~1000px wide (PASS) / ~1100–1300px tall (FILM) as hero; action rail docked bottom, subordinate.
- **Proof Lock** — registration corners converge, serial locks, rail dissolves, PAPER CUT to Printing.
- **Printing** — ISSUE CORE (210–300px) fused with the printer mouth; paper feeds; core states legible.
- **Done** — finished artefact maximized, connected to the collect direction as a full instruction rail, not a footnote.
- **Error Recovery / Staff Mode / Inactivity** — same registration grammar (owner already rebuilt Inactivity into an editorial "session hold"; Error & Staff to be unified to match).
